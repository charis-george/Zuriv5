# Zuri Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

**Last updated:** March 2026

---

## Important: We Do Not Hold Your Money

**Zuri does not collect, store, or hold customer funds.** We only facilitate payments through licensed third-party payment processors (such as Paystack). Card and payment details are processed directly by those providers. Payouts to businesses are made by the payment processor to the business’s own bank account. This means Zuri operates as a software platform for menus, orders, and payment facilitation—not as a custodian of funds—and is not subject to fintech regulations that apply to entities that hold or transmit customer funds.

---

## 1. Welcome

Zuri provides digital ordering and payment facilitation for businesses (restaurants, cafés, events, etc.). Businesses use Zuri to publish menus, receive orders, and get paid; customers use Zuri to browse, order, and pay. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal data and the terms that apply when you use Zuri.

**Roles:** Depending on context, Zuri may act as a data controller (e.g. for your account, support, and platform operations) or as a data processor (e.g. when processing order and customer data on behalf of a business).

---

## 2. Personal Data We Collect

- **Account data:** For businesses: name, email, password (hashed), phone, address, bank account details for payouts (held securely; used only to instruct the payment processor). For Zuri Tap customers: email, password (hashed), optional name and phone.
- **Order and transaction data:** Order details, amounts, status, and payment status. We do not store full card numbers; payment data is handled by the licensed payment processor.
- **Usage data:** Logs, device/browser information, and interaction data necessary to run and secure the service.

---

## 3. How We Use Personal Data

We use personal data to: provide and operate the Zuri platform; facilitate orders and payments via third-party processors; provide analytics and insights to businesses; improve the product; prevent fraud and protect security; and comply with law.

---

## 4. How We Share Personal Data

We may share data with: **payment processors** (to complete transactions); **hosting and infrastructure providers** (e.g. Supabase); **the business** you order from or whose storefront you use; and **legal authorities** when required. We do not sell personal data.

---

## 5. Payment Processing & No Custody of Funds

Payments are processed entirely by licensed third-party payment providers. Zuri does not collect, store, or hold customer funds. We do not store full card numbers or sensitive payment credentials. Payouts to businesses are executed by the payment processor to the business’s designated bank account. Your use of Zuri is subject to the payment provider’s terms and privacy policy where applicable.

---

## 6. Data Security & Retention

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data. Data is retained only as long as needed to provide the service, comply with law, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to certain processing of your data. Contact us (below) to exercise these rights.
